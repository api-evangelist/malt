The Problem: Context Is Finite, and It Fills Up Fast Every conversation with Claude has a fixed context window — a hard ceiling on how much information can exist in its working memory at once. On a simple project, this is rarely an issue. On a monorepo with dozens of services, hundreds of files, and deep cross-cutting concerns, you’ll hit the ceiling faster than you’d expect.
