TL;DR: Getting early adopters to use AI is easy; getting the rest of the company on board is a different sport. To jump from 55% to 90% adoption, we had to look at the human reality: daily frustrations, lack of time, and the underlying fear of being replaced. We abandoned generic presentations for highly customized, hands-on workshops based on actual workflows.
