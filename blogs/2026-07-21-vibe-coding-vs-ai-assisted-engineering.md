Stop throwing “vibe coding” around when you talk to an engineer Since early 2025, one word is everywhere, usually dropped with a little smirk: vibe coding . And nine times out of ten (fine, “most of the time”) the person saying it actually means any use of AI to write code . That’s wrong.
